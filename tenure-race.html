Tenure runs from a player's first game with the franchise to the day shown. A player counts as present while his stint spans that date, so summers are held rather than reset — and a player who left and came back starts again from the return. This is where players played: a signing who never got off the bench leaves no trace, and an arrival dates from his debut rather than from the trade.
Built from data/timelines.ndjson. Ties are common — a draft class debuts together — and are broken by who ends up staying longer.
